About Us

Get to know Andromeda Event Enterprise, an exhibition information and publication platform that helps connect exhibitors, products, and business opportunities in one accessible information space.

01

Getting to Know Andromeda

Andromeda Event Enterprise is here to provide information and expand the publication reach of various exhibition events.

Through product and exhibitor introductions, we help visitors discover various companies, products, and business opportunities showcased at exhibitions.

In addition to providing information, Andromeda also offers exhibitor information services and product auction activities through TikTok Live.

Connecting Business Opportunities

Creating an information space that brings together products, companies, exhibitors, and exhibition visitors to open up opportunities for connections and business collaborations.

02

Vision & Mission

Vision

To become an exhibition information and publication platform that helps connect business owners, products, exhibitors, and visitors through accessible and valuable information.

Mission

  • Introducing featured products from various exhibition participants.
  • Providing exhibitor profiles and identification information.
  • Making information more accessible to visitors and potential business partners.
  • Providing exhibition publications and product auction activities.
  • Building connections and business opportunities through exhibition information.
